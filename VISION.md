## Core Identity
- **Tagline:** "The smartest way to travel. Less money, less searching, more vibes."
- **What it is:** AI-powered travel web app that finds the cheapest possible way to take any trip
- **Key differentiator:** Optimizes across flights AND trains simultaneously

## Three Defensible Edges
1. **Price optimization engine** — multi-city route ordering + flight vs train comparison (door-to-door time)
2. **Flowchart trip view** — visual left-to-right city cards with animated connectors showing transport mode, time, price
3. **Group workspace** — real-time shared trip planning, viral growth engine

## Three Ways to Start a Trip
1. **Destination-first** — user names cities, Voyza optimizes routing
2. **Budget-first** — user sets budget, Voyza suggests reachable destinations
3. **Vibe-first** — user describes a feeling, Voyza matches destinations

## Screen Flow
1. Welcome screen (dark, plane animation, VOYZA logo, single input)
2. Planning flow (conversational step-by-step OR open chat input)
3. Flowchart results page (city cards L→R, connectors, AI chat panel on right)
4. City detail tab (day-by-day view, activities, restaurants, bookable transport)
5. Group workspace (shared real-time view, permissions, contributions)
6. Trip history and sharing (stored cards, shareable completed trips)

## Business Model
- Affiliate commissions (~60%) on flights, trains, hotels
- Premium subscription (~30%) at $7.99/mo or $49/yr
- Sponsored deal placements (~10%)

## Free vs Premium
- Free: conversational planning, basic optimization, flight+train search, flowchart view, group up to 3, trip history
- Premium: full multi-city optimizer, deal alerts, unlimited group, price lock, AI rebooking, offline access, flash deals
