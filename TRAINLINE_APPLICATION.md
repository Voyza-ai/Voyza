## "Additional information" — paste this

> Voyza is an AI-powered, multi-modal trip planner (web app) that finds travelers
> the cheapest way to take a trip by optimizing routes across **flights and
> trains simultaneously** — comparing door-to-door time and price on every leg of
> a multi-city itinerary.
>
> Rail is core to our product, not an add-on. We are **Europe-first**: our
> optimizer's value depends on accurate, bookable rail content across mainland
> Europe. We currently use Duffel for flights and the community Deutsche Bahn
> REST API for trains — but DB's coverage is effectively Germany-only, which
> leaves us blind to SNCF, Trenitalia, Renfe, CP (Portugal) and others. That gap
> is exactly what we're looking to close.
>
> We're seeking a **Global API** integration to (1) surface real fares and
> schedules across France, Spain, Italy, Germany, Benelux, Switzerland, Austria
> and Portugal, and (2) longer term, enable booking. We are early-stage and
> consumer-facing (end users are individual leisure travelers), and we'd welcome
> a conversation about the right entry point — affiliate, API, or a staged path —
> for a fast-growing multi-modal travel product.
>
> Happy to share a product demo and our roadmap. Thank you!

## If Trainline is slow / declines (run in parallel)

1. **Trainline affiliate programme** — lower bar than full API; gets us booking
   deep-links + commission immediately while we pursue API access. Linked from
   their [Partnerships page](https://www.thetrainline.com/about-us/partnerships).
2. **Omio Partner** — 1,000+ operators, multimodal (train+bus+flight). Apply in
   parallel as a hedge.
3. **Per-operator open data** — SNCF and some others publish developer APIs;
   fragmented but free, usable for schedules even without booking.
4. **Static tables (already underway)** — our Phase 1b stopgap covers the top
   ~50 EU corridors now, independent of any partnership timeline.
